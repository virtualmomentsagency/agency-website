About VMA

A founder-led team behind every placement.

We started Virtual Moments Agency to help business owners focus on what truly matters, by taking care of the details that keep a business running.

The VMA story

Built on a simple idea: you should get your time back.

Founded in January 2024, VMA grew quickly into a trusted partner for entrepreneurs, professionals and small businesses, and today focuses on medspa owners who need their front desk covered.

We maintain a select network of virtual partners who share our values, and match each one to a client's specific needs. Whether it's managing your calendar, answering inquiries or streamlining your workflow, we're with you every step of the way.
